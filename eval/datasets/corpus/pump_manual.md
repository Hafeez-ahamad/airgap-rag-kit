# P-100 Pump Manual

## 1 Maintenance
The P-100 pump must be serviced every 500 operating hours.
Use ISO VG 46 hydraulic oil only. Replace the inlet filter at every second service.

## 2 Safety
Isolate the electrical supply before opening the pump housing.
Wear eye protection when bleeding the hydraulic circuit.

## 3 Troubleshooting
If the pressure gauge reads below 2 bar at full speed, check the inlet filter for blockage first.
